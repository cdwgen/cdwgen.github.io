# Sampling Locations

The final dataset consisted of 2,894 wild *Oncorhynchus* specimens from the Kootenai Basin, 137 hatchery CRT, 56 hatchery IRT, 60 putative YCT, and 402 *Oncorhynchus* specimens from Alberta, CA hatchery lines and the Athabasca River system. The sampling map below includes all locations sampled between 2003 and 2024 within the Kootenai basin. Some locations (East Fork Yaak River, Bear Creek, Wolf Creek, etc.) were sampled multiple years in a row in the same location so some points may overlap. Sampling locations outside the Kootenai basin are not shown, but complete sample metadata can be found [here]().
