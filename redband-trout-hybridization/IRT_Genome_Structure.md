# Genome Structure of Kootenai Redband Trout

This section describes the large-scale genomic structure of Kootenai River Basin Redband Trout, with an emphasis on chromosome organization, linkage map concordance, and structural variation relevant to hybridization with Cutthroat and Rainbow Trout lineages. The figures below summarize genome-wide patterns and chromosome-scale features derived from linkage mapping and comparative genomics.
